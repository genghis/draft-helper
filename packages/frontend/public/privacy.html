Draft Helper Sync — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Draft Helper Sync is a Chrome extension that mirrors picks from your ESPN fantasy football draft room onto your own Draft Helper board in real time. It is a private tool for one fantasy league. This policy explains exactly what it handles.

What the extension accesses

  • Draft picks from the page you have open. While you are in an ESPN draft room (fantasy.espn.com/football/draft*), the extension reads the picks as they happen — the player selected, the pick number, and which team made the pick. It does not read anything else on the page and is inactive on every other site.
  • A sync token you paste in. You generate this token in Draft Helper and paste it into the extension once. It identifies your account so picks land on your board.

What it does with that data

  • Picks are sent, over HTTPS, only to the Draft Helper API at draft.clanseafox.com — the app you already use. They are stored on your own Draft Helper board so you can see them there.
  • The token is stored locally in your browser's extension storage and sent with those requests to authenticate you. Nothing else is transmitted.

What it does not do

  • No advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party services.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No access to your ESPN login, password, or account — it only reads pick data shown on the draft page.
  • No browsing history, and no activity on any site other than the ESPN draft room.

Data retention and removal

Draft picks live on your Draft Helper board until you clear them (the app's “Reset draft” action). Remove the extension to delete the stored token from your browser; revoke it any time under Draft Helper → Settings.
